Community

Growing, one partnership at a time

CyberSafe Connect is being built as more than a website — the goal is a youth community around digital safety, where students, educators, schools, youth organizations, and technology communities work together to build safer digital environments. That community doesn't exist yet at scale — it's being built one conversation, one workshop, and one partnership at a time.

About

About CyberSafe Connect

CyberSafe Connect began as an independent youth project exploring how digital-safety education can become more practical, accessible, and engaging for young people. It was independently founded and developed by Fatimaezzahra Hrimech, and is being built as a real, usable initiative — one that can grow through workshops, schools, and partnerships rather than staying a static website.

Why It Matters

Why SDG 17?

Meaningful social impact rarely happens in isolation. It takes partnerships — between young people, schools, NGOs, universities, governments, businesses, and communities — to turn an idea into something that actually changes how people live and stay safe online. CyberSafe Connect is built on that principle.

About

About CyberSafe Connect

CyberSafe Connect is a youth-focused program that brings together digital safety, technology, collaboration, and social impact. It's designed as a full arc: learn cybersecurity, test your judgment against real scenarios, apply what you know, then build an actual community project or campaign — the thinking at the heart of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goal 17.

    Why It Matters

    Why SDG 17?

    Meaningful social impact rarely happens in isolation. It takes partnerships — between young people, schools, NGOs, universities, governments, businesses, and communities — to turn an idea into something that actually changes how people live and stay safe online. CyberSafe Connect is built on that principle: cybersecurity awareness becomes far more powerful when it's shared, taught, and built together.

    About

    About CyberSafe Connect

    CyberSafe Connect is a youth-focused concept that brings together digital safety, technology, collaboration, and social impact in one platform. It's designed to help young people understand and practice good cybersecurity habits, while also giving them a space to imagine and structure real community partnerships — the kind of thinking at the heart of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goal 17.
